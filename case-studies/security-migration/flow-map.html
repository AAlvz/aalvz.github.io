What the watchers did

51 small background scripts did the waiting and checking, so nobody had to refresh GitLab. They only read and reported; the one thing they were allowed to do was retry a job once.

  1. Waited for rebases to finishbefore trusting any pipeline result
  2. Followed each MR's pipelinethe one for its latest commit, job by job, until green or failed
  3. Retried runner failures oncea job that died on the runner itself, not on the code (3 MRs)
  4. Checked the reported image is the real oneimage pushed by the build = image announced to the scanner (caught 5 repos reporting test images)
  5. Held approved Slack messagessent a team's message only once all its MRs were green (two teams)
  6. Checked the first pipeline after mergethe scanner said "Scanning image" with the right arch
