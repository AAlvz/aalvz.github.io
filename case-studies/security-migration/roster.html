Security Migration Agents

A platform team had to move about a hundred repositories onto a centrally enforced security-scanning pipeline. One engineer did it with a small crew of AI agents and scripts. These are the agents, one card per job: click a card, or press Play tour, to see its instructions and the rules added as real repos surprised us.

Counts come from the migration's session records. Background jobs are small scripts, not AI agents. The per-repo steps ran inside each group agent. Company, team, repo and channel names are changed.
